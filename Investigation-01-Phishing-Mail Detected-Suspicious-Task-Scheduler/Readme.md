## 📌 Incident Summary

A phishing email with the subject **"COVID19 Vaccine"** was detected by the email security gateway. The email originated from **aaronluo@cmail.carleton.ca** and contained a malicious PDF attachment named **material.pdf**.

The attachment was analyzed using **VirusTotal** and **ANY.RUN**, where it was identified as malicious. However, the email gateway successfully blocked the message before it reached the recipient, preventing the attachment from being opened or executed. No malicious activity or endpoint compromise was observed.

---
